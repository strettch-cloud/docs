---
title: "Managing DNS"
description: "Add a domain to your Strettch Cloud team, point it to our nameservers, and add, check and delete its DNS records."
---

You need to be an Owner or an Admin of the team to add or remove domains and records.

## Add a domain

You need a domain that you bought from a registrar. Strettch Cloud does not sell domains.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the setup">
    Go to **Domains** and click **Setup Domain**. When the team has no domain yet, the button reads **Set up Domain**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect your domain">
    Type your domain, for example `example.com`, and click **Validate**. If the domain cannot be used, a message says why. When it is valid, click **Continue**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Imported DNS Records">
    This step appears only when we find records that your domain already has. Choose **Yes, Import Records** to copy them, select the ones you want and click **Import Selected Records**. Choose **I'll do it later** to start with no records.
  </Step>

  <Step title="DNS Delegation">
    The page shows our two nameservers, `ns1.strettch.com` and `ns2.strettch.com`. Log in at your registrar and replace the domain's nameservers with these two. See [Provider guides](/services/dns/dns-provider-guides) for the steps at some registrars. Then click **Continue**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Active Domain">
    Click **Activate Domain**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The domain is now in the **Domains** list with the status **ACTIVATING...**. It becomes **ACTIVE** when we see our nameservers on it, which takes between 3 minutes and 24 hours. If it shows **INACTIVE**, check the nameservers at your registrar. See [How long a new domain takes to become active](/services/dns/overview#how-long-a-new-domain-takes-to-become-active).

You can add records only when the domain is **ACTIVE**.

## Add a record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the domain">
    Go to **Domains** and click the domain. The tabs on the side filter its records by type.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start a new record">
    Click **Add New Record**. On the **ALL** tab, choose the **Record Type** first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the record">
    The fields depend on the type. Every type has a name, which is **Hostname** for most types, a value, and **TTL (Seconds)**. MX and SRV records also have a **Priority**.

    In **Hostname**, type only the part before your domain. For `www.example.com`, type `www`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the record">
    Click **Add Record**. The record is served immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Record types](/services/dns/overview#record-types) for what each type does, and [TTL](/services/dns/overview#ttl-and-how-fast-a-change-takes-effect) for which value to choose.

## Check a record

Ask for the record with `dig`. The `+short` option prints only the value.

<CodeGroup>
```bash A record
dig A api.example.com +short
```

```bash MX record
dig MX example.com +short
```

```bash TXT record
dig TXT _dmarc.example.com +short
```

```bash CNAME record
dig CNAME blog.example.com +short
```
</CodeGroup>

You should see the value you entered. If you see an old value, wait until its TTL has run out and ask again.

## Change a record

The dashboard cannot edit a record. Delete the record and add it again with the new value.

## Delete a record

A deleted record cannot be restored.

1. Go to **Domains** and click the domain.
2. On the row of the record, open the menu and click **Delete Record**.
3. In the dialog, click **Delete**.

## Remove a domain

Removing a domain removes it and its records from Strettch Cloud DNS. The domain stays yours at your registrar. If the registrar still points it to our nameservers, the domain stops resolving, so change its nameservers there first.

1. Go to **Domains**.
2. On the row of the domain, open the menu and click **Remove Domain**.
3. In the dialog, click **Remove Domain**.
