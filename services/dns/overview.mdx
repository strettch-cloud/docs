Strettch Cloud DNS hosts the DNS records of a domain you already own. You point the domain to our nameservers at the company where you registered it, and from then on you manage its records in the dashboard.

We do not sell domain names. Buy the domain from a registrar first, then [add it to your team](/services/dns/managing-dns#add-a-domain).

## Our nameservers

Every domain on Strettch Cloud uses the same two nameservers:

- `ns1.strettch.com`
- `ns2.strettch.com`

A domain works with our DNS only while your registrar lists these two as its nameservers.

## How long a new domain takes to become active

After you set our nameservers at your registrar and activate the domain, it takes between 3 minutes and 24 hours before the rest of the internet follows the change. The time depends on your registrar.

The **Domains** list shows where each domain stands:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **ACTIVATING...** | We do not see our nameservers on the domain yet |
| **ACTIVE** | The domain uses our nameservers and its records are served |
| **INACTIVE** | After 24 hours we still do not see our nameservers. Check them at your registrar |

A domain that is still not active after 14 days is removed from your team. You can add it again.

## Record types

| Type | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Points a name to an IPv4 address | `example.com` to `192.0.2.1` |
| **AAAA** | Points a name to an IPv6 address | `example.com` to `2001:db8::1` |
| **CNAME** | Makes one name an alias of another | `blog.example.com` to `example.com` |
| **MX** | Names the mail server of a domain | `example.com` to `mail.example.com` |
| **TXT** | Holds text, such as a verification string or an SPF or DMARC policy | `v=DMARC1; p=none` |
| **NS** | Hands a subdomain over to other nameservers | `api.example.com` to another DNS provider |
| **SRV** | Says where a service runs | `_sip._tcp.example.com` |
| **CAA** | Says which certificate authorities may issue certificates for the domain | `letsencrypt.org` |

## TTL and how fast a change takes effect

A record change is on our nameservers immediately. Other resolvers keep the old value until its TTL runs out.

The TTL of a record is the number of seconds a resolver may keep the answer. The form fills in 3600 seconds, which is one hour, and accepts 60 to 86400 seconds. Before a change that you may need to undo quickly, add the record with a low TTL.

## Subdomains

To use a subdomain such as `api.example.com`, add a record for it to `example.com`. You do not add the subdomain as a domain of its own.

You cannot add `project.example.com` as its own domain on Strettch Cloud while `example.com` is also on our nameservers. Two other setups work:

- **The parent is on Strettch Cloud, the subdomain elsewhere.** Add NS records for `project` to `example.com` that point to the other provider's nameservers.
- **The parent is elsewhere, the subdomain on Strettch Cloud.** Add `project.example.com` as a domain here, and at the provider of `example.com` add NS records for `project` that point to `ns1.strettch.com` and `ns2.strettch.com`.
