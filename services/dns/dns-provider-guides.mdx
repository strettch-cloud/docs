---
title: "Change your nameservers at your domain provider"
sidebarTitle: "Provider Guides"
description: "Steps to point a domain to the Strettch Cloud nameservers at Vercel, Cloudflare, Bunny.net and Namecheap."
---

Change the nameservers at the company where you registered the domain. These are the steps for four providers. At any other provider, look for the nameserver settings of the domain and enter the same two nameservers:

- `ns1.strettch.com`
- `ns2.strettch.com`

A nameserver change can take up to 24 hours to take effect. When you have made it, go back to the dashboard and [activate the domain](/services/dns/managing-dns#add-a-domain).

## Vercel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the domain">
    In your Vercel dashboard, select the project that uses the domain, or open your team settings if the domain belongs to the team. Go to the **Domains** tab and click the domain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch to custom nameservers">
    Scroll down to the **Nameservers** section. It is set to "Vercel Nameservers". Switch to **Custom Nameservers**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the nameservers">
    Enter `ns1.strettch.com` and `ns2.strettch.com`, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cloudflare

These steps apply when you registered the domain with Cloudflare. If you only use Cloudflare for DNS and registered the domain somewhere else, change the nameservers at that registrar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the domain">
    Log in to your Cloudflare dashboard and select your domain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the nameserver settings">
    Go to **Domain Registration** > **Manage Domain** > **Configuration** and look for **Custom Nameservers**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the nameservers">
    Replace the existing nameservers with `ns1.strettch.com` and `ns2.strettch.com`, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bunny.net

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the domain">
    Log in to Bunny.net and go to **Services** > **Domain Registration**. Click your domain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose custom nameservers">
    Click the **Nameservers** tab in the left menu and select **Use custom nameservers**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the nameservers">
    Enter `ns1.strettch.com` as nameserver 1 and `ns2.strettch.com` as nameserver 2, then click **Update Nameservers**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Namecheap

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the domain">
    Log in to your Namecheap account and go to **Domain List**. Click **Manage** next to your domain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose custom DNS">
    In the **Nameservers** section, select **Custom DNS** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the nameservers">
    Enter `ns1.strettch.com` and `ns2.strettch.com` on the two lines, then click the green checkmark to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>
