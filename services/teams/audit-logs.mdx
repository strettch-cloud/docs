The activity logs record what happens in a team: who did what, to which resource, and when. Each team has its own logs.

## Where the activity logs are

Go to **Team Settings** > **Activity Logs**. Every member of the team can read them.

Each entry is one sentence that says who did what. Click an entry to see its details: the actor, the action, the resource, the outcome, where the request came from and when.

Use **Search logs** to find entries.

## What is logged

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Sign-ins** | A member logged in or out, or a login failed |
| **Computes** | A compute was created, turned off, rebooted, upsized or destroyed |
| **DNS** | A domain was added or removed, a record was added or deleted |
| **SSH keys** | A key was added, edited or deleted |
| **API keys** | A key was generated or deleted |
| **Members** | A member was invited or removed, a role was changed |
| **Ownership** | Ownership was transferred |
| **Team** | The team was renamed, its settings were changed |
| **Billing** | Funds were added, an invoice was created, credits were received |

## Export the activity logs

You need to be an Owner or an Admin.

Click **Export**, choose a format and a period, and click **Export Logs**.
