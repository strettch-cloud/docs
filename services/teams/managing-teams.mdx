---

Most of these tasks need a role in the team. See [What each role can do](/services/teams/overview#what-each-role-can-do).

## Create a team

1. Click the team selector, next to the logo at the top of the dashboard, and click **New Team**.
2. In the **Create a new team** dialog, type a **Team Name** and choose a colour for its avatar.
3. Click **Create Team**.

You are the Owner of the new team. It has its own computes, domains and bill.

## Switch between teams

Click the team selector and choose a team. The dashboard then shows the computes, domains and billing of that team.

## Invite a member

You need to be an Owner or an Admin.

1. Go to **Team Settings** > **Members** and click **Invite Members**.
2. Type the person's **Email address** and choose a role, Member or Admin.
3. Click **Invite**.

The person receives an email with the invitation, and you can also copy the invitation link and send it yourself. An invitation is valid for 10 days.

Until the person accepts, the invitation is listed on the **PENDING INVITATIONS** tab. From its menu you can change the role with **Update Role** or withdraw it with **Cancel Invitation**.

To accept an invitation, the person opens **Account Settings** > **TEAMS** > **PENDING INVITATIONS** and clicks **Accept**.

## Change a member's role

You need to be an Owner or an Admin. You cannot change your own role or the Owner's.

1. Go to **Team Settings** > **Members**.
2. Open the menu on the member's row and click **Update Role**.
3. Choose the new role and click **Save Changes**.

The new role applies at once.

## Remove a member

You need to be an Owner or an Admin. You cannot remove the Owner.

Removing a member takes away their access to the dashboard, but not to your computes. Before you remove someone:

- delete their SSH keys from `~/.ssh/authorized_keys` on every compute they could log in to;
- change every root password they know.

Then remove them from the team:

1. Go to **Team Settings** > **Members**.
2. Open the menu on the member's row and click **Remove Member**.
3. Tick the two statements in the dialog, which confirm that you did both things above, and click **Yes, Remove member**.

The member loses access to the team at once, and the API keys they created for the team stop working. This cannot be undone, but you can invite the person again.

## Transfer ownership

Only the Owner can do this, and the new Owner must already be a member of the team. You cannot transfer the only team you own.

1. Go to **Team Settings** > **Members**.
2. Open the menu on the member's row and click **Transfer Ownership**.
3. In the **Transfer Team Ownership** dialog, click **Transfer Ownership**.

The member must accept before the transfer happens. Until then you stay the Owner, and their row shows **TRANSFER PENDING**. When they accept, they become the Owner and you become an Admin. Only the new Owner can transfer the team again.

## Leave a team

An Admin or a Member can leave. The Owner cannot: transfer ownership first, or delete the team.

1. Go to **Team Settings** > **General**.
2. Under **Leave Team**, click **Leave Team** and confirm.

You lose access to the team at once.

## Delete a team

Only the Owner can delete a team, and deleting cannot be undone. Every member loses access.

Before you can delete a team:

- it is not the only team you own;
- it has no computes left, so [destroy them](/services/compute/destroy-a-compute) first;
- it has no active domains, so [remove them](/services/dns/managing-dns#remove-a-domain) first;
- it owes nothing and holds no more than RWF 10,000 in funds. If it holds more, [contact support](/support).

1. Go to **Team Settings** > **General**.
2. Under **Delete Team**, click **Delete Team**.
3. In the **Delete Team?** dialog, click **Delete Team**.
