Every compute comes with a monthly allowance of outbound internet traffic. The allowances of all the computes in a team are added together into one pool, and you only pay for traffic above that pool.

## What Is Measured

Bandwidth is the **outbound internet traffic** sent by your computes: data leaving our network for the internet, such as responses your applications send to visitors and files they download from you.

These are **not** counted and are always free:

- **Inbound traffic**, such as uploads to your computes and packages you download onto them.
- **Traffic between your computes**, and any traffic that stays inside our network.

Traffic is measured on the server that hosts your compute, not inside the compute, so it is counted the same way whatever runs on it.

## Allowance per Specification

Each specification includes a monthly allowance:

| vCPU | RAM | Monthly allowance |
|------|-----|-------------------|
| 1 | 512 MB | 115 GB |
| 1 | 1 GB | 235 GB |
| 1 | 2 GB | 470 GB |
| 2 | 4 GB | 900 GB |
| 4 | 8 GB | 1,800 GB |
| 8 | 16 GB | 3,600 GB |
| 16 | 32 GB | 7,200 GB |

The allowance for each specification is also shown in the **Bandwidth** column when you create a compute. 1 GB is 1,000,000,000 bytes.

## How the Team Allowance Works

**The allowance is pooled per team.** Every compute adds its allowance to one team-wide amount, and your bill depends on the team's total traffic against that amount. One busy compute can use the allowance of quieter ones.

**Part of a month counts for part of the allowance.** A compute adds its allowance for the share of the month it exists. For example, a 1 vCPU / 2 GB compute created halfway through a 30-day month adds about 235 GB, not 470 GB. A compute you destroy keeps the share it built up until it was destroyed.

**Stopped computes still count.** A stopped compute is still billed, so it keeps adding to the allowance.

**Upsizing gives you the bigger allowance for that whole month.** If you upsize a compute, the new specification's allowance applies from the start of the month you upsized in. Past months are not changed.

**Months follow UTC.** A month starts at 00:00 UTC on the 1st, which is 02:00 in Kigali.

## Price

Traffic above your team's allowance costs **38 RWF per GB**.

## How It Is Charged

Bandwidth is charged the same way as your computes: daily, from your credits first and then your balance.

- **Each day**, we work out your team's usage for the month so far, take away the team allowance and what you have already been charged this month, and charge the rest.
- **Charges are in steps of 0.01 GB**, rounded down. Anything below that carries over to the next day, so nothing is charged twice and nothing is lost.
- **On your monthly invoice**, bandwidth has its own **Bandwidth** section with one line, **Extra Bandwidth Usage**, showing the GB above your allowance, the price per GB and the amount.

If your team stays within its allowance, there is no bandwidth charge and no bandwidth line on the invoice.

## Where to See Your Usage

**For the whole team:** go to **Team Settings > Billing > Overview** and open the **Bandwidth** tab. It shows the team's usage against its allowance for the selected month, any extra usage and its cost, and a table of each compute's usage and included allowance.

**For one compute:** open the compute and find the **Outbound Bandwidth** chart. It shows the compute's usage for the month day by day, with a dashed line for the allowance this compute adds to the team.

<Info>
Usage figures cover full days, up to the end of the previous day in UTC. Today's traffic appears tomorrow.
</Info>

<Warning>
A compute going past its own line on the chart does not mean you are charged. Charges only apply when the **team's** total goes above the **team's** allowance.
</Warning>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I pay for traffic coming into my computes?">
    No. Only outbound internet traffic counts. Inbound traffic is always free.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is traffic between my computes counted?">
    No. Traffic that stays inside our network, including traffic between your computes, is free and does not use your allowance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does unused allowance carry over to the next month?">
    No. Each month starts with a new allowance based on the computes in your team that month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get a bigger allowance?">
    Upsize a compute or add computes to your team. A bigger specification gives the bigger allowance for the whole month you upsize in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
