A compute is a virtual machine that runs Linux, with root access for you. You pay for a compute for as long as it exists, from the moment you create it until you destroy it, whether it is running or turned off.

## What you choose for a compute

- **An image**: a Linux system, or an application image with an application already installed.
- **A specification**: one combination of CPU, RAM and storage. The price of each specification is shown when you create a compute.
- **A public IP**, if the internet must reach the compute. It is billed separately. See [Public IPs and the private network](/services/compute/public-ips-and-the-private-network).
- **How you log in**: an [SSH key](/services/compute/ssh-keys) or a root password.

A compute belongs to a [team](/services/teams/overview), and the team pays for it.

## What you pay in each state

The price of a compute is per hour. We count the hours a compute exists each day and round them up to a full hour.

For example, at 10 RWF per hour, a compute that exists for a whole day costs 240 RWF for that day. It costs the same whether it is busy, idle or turned off.

| State | What it means | Can you connect? | Billed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STARTING** | The compute is being created or turned on | No | Yes |
| **RUNNING** | The compute is on | Yes | Yes |
| **REBOOTING** | The compute is restarting | No | Yes |
| **STOPPING**, **STOPPED** | The compute is turned off. It keeps its disk and its IP addresses | No | Yes, at the same price |
| **DESTROYING**, **DESTROYED** | The compute is gone and cannot be brought back | No | No |

<Warning>
  A compute that is turned off is still billed at the same price, because its CPU, RAM and storage stay reserved for you. Billing stops only when you [destroy the compute](/services/compute/destroy-a-compute).
</Warning>

You see the state of each compute in the **Computes** list. **Total Charged (For This Month)** on a compute's page shows what it has cost so far this month.

See [Paying your bill](/faqs/paying-your-bill) for when the charges are taken.

## How long a compute takes to create

A new compute is usually running in less than a minute. The web console needs a little longer. See [SC Agent](/agents/sc-agent#when-the-web-console-is-ready).

## Limits for a team

A team has a limit on the number of computes, the number of public IPs and the size of one compute. You see them in **Team Settings** > **General**, under **Resource Limits**. Click **Request More** there to ask for a higher limit.

## Bandwidth

Each compute includes a monthly allowance of outbound internet traffic, and the allowances of all the computes in a team are pooled. Inbound traffic and traffic between your computes are free. You pay only for outbound traffic above the team's allowance. See [Bandwidth](/services/compute/bandwidth).
