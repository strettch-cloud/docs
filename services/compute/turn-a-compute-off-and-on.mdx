---
title: "Turn a compute off and on"
description: "Turn a compute off, turn it on again or reboot it from the dashboard. A compute that is turned off is still billed."
---

You need to be an Owner or an Admin of the team.

<Warning>
  A compute that is turned off is still billed at the same price. To stop billing, [destroy the compute](/services/compute/destroy-a-compute).
</Warning>

## Turn a compute off

When you turn a compute off from the dashboard, we first try a graceful shutdown. If that fails, we force the shutdown, and a forced shutdown can corrupt data. To be safe, shut the operating system down from inside the compute first:

```bash
poweroff
```

Then turn the compute off in the dashboard:

1. In the **Computes** list, click the compute.
2. Open the **Power** tab and click **Turn Off**.
3. In the **Turn Off Compute** dialog, click **Turn off**.

The compute changes to **STOPPED**. It keeps its data and its IP addresses.

## Turn a compute on

1. In the **Computes** list, click the compute.
2. Open the **Power** tab and click **Turn On**.
3. In the **Turn On Compute** dialog, click **Turn on**.

The compute changes to **RUNNING**.

The **On** / **Off** switch at the top of the compute's page does the same as the two buttons.

## Reboot a compute

A reboot stops the compute and starts it again. It breaks every open connection, and the compute is back in about 2 minutes.

1. In the **Computes** list, click the compute. It must be running.
2. At the top of the page, click the reboot button, next to the **On** switch.
3. In the **Reboot Compute?** dialog, click **Reboot**.
