---
title: "Create a compute"
description: "Create a compute from the dashboard: choose a location, an image, a specification, a public IP and how you log in."
---

Your team needs enough credits and funds to cover the **Total (Cost per month)** that the summary shows for the compute. If it does not, the dashboard asks you to [top up](/faqs/paying-your-bill) before it creates the compute.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new compute">
    Go to **Computes** and click **Create Compute**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Location">
    Choose where the compute runs. Pick the location closest to the people who will use it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Image">
    Choose what is installed on the compute. Each image is marked **OS** or **Application**.

    - An OS image is a Linux system with nothing else on it. Choose its version from the list on the card.
    - An application image comes with an application already installed. **See Details** opens the page that says what is on it and how to start it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Type">
    Choose a specification from the table. Each row is one combination of CPU, RAM and storage, with its monthly [bandwidth allowance](/services/compute/bandwidth) and its price per hour and per month.

    A specification that is too small for the image you chose cannot be selected. You can [upsize](/services/compute/upsize-a-compute) a compute later, but you cannot make it smaller.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Networking Options">
    **Assign a Public IP Address** is turned on. A public IP is billed separately from the compute, at the price shown under the switch. Turn the switch off if nothing on the internet needs to reach this compute.

    You cannot add or remove a public IP after the compute is created. See [Public IPs and the private network](/services/compute/public-ips-and-the-private-network) to decide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authentication Method">
    Choose how you will log in as `root`. You choose one of the two, not both.

    - **SSH Key**: select one or more of your team's SSH keys, or click **New SSH Key** to add one. See [SSH Keys](/services/compute/ssh-keys).
    - **Root Password**: type a password that meets the rules shown under the field. Store it before you go on. We do not email it, and the dashboard does not show it again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compute Name">
    A name is filled in for you. Keep it or type your own.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Tags (Optional)">
    Add tags if you want to group your computes, for example by environment. You can change them later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the compute">
    Check the **Summary**, then click **Create Compute**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You are taken back to the **Computes** list, and a message shows the SSH command for the new compute. The compute shows **STARTING...** and then **RUNNING**. You can open it once it is running.
