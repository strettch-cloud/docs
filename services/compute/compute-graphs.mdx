title: "Compute graphs"
description: "What the six graphs on a compute's Monitoring tab show, what to watch for in each, and where a compute's monthly bandwidth usage is."
---

Every compute has six graphs of its CPU, memory, disk, network and load. They are on the **Monitoring** tab, which opens first when you click a compute in the **Computes** list.

## What each graph shows

| Graph | What it shows | What to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| **CPU Usage (%)** | How much of the compute's CPU is in use | Usage that stays above 80% means the compute needs more CPU. |
| **Memory** | How much of the compute's RAM is in use, as a percentage | Usage that stays above 90% means the compute needs more RAM. |
| **Disk Usage** | How much of the compute's storage is full, as a percentage | A disk that fills up stops applications from writing data. |
| **Disk IOPS** | Read and write operations on the disk | Long periods of heavy disk activity slow applications down. |
| **Network** | Public and private traffic, inbound and outbound | A spike you do not expect can mean unusual traffic. |
| **Load (1/5/15)** | The system load average over 1, 5 and 15 minutes | A load higher than the number of CPUs means processes are waiting for CPU. |

If a compute stays short of CPU or RAM, you can [upsize it](/services/compute/upsize-a-compute).

## Choose the period

**Period** sets how far back the graphs go, from the last hour to the last 30 days. Hover over a graph to see the value at one point in time. **Refresh** loads the newest values.

## Bandwidth used this month

Below the **Network** graph, **Egress bandwidth usage this month** shows how much outbound internet traffic the compute has sent this month. **View Team Usage** opens the usage of the whole team.

One compute using more than its own allowance is not a charge by itself. You pay only when the whole team goes above its pooled allowance. See [Bandwidth](/services/compute/bandwidth).

## Where the graphs get their data

The [SC Metrics Agent](/agents/sc-metrics-agent) runs on the compute and sends the values to the dashboard. It is installed on every compute you create. If you stop or uninstall it, the graphs stay empty.
