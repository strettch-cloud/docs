Two ideas explain most of Strettch Cloud. Everything you create belongs to a team. And you pay for the time a resource exists, not for how hard it works.

## Everything belongs to a team

A compute or a domain belongs to one team, and that team pays for it. There is no layer between a team and its resources, such as a project.

When you sign up, we create a team for you. You can create more teams and be a member of other people's teams. Teams do not share resources or bills.

Each member of a team has a role: Owner, Admin or Member. See [Teams overview](/services/teams/overview) for what each role can do.

## You pay for the time a resource exists

A compute is billed for every hour it exists, at the price of its specification. It costs the same whether it works all day or sits idle. Nothing scales up or down by itself, so the price does not change with load.

<Warning>
  Turning a compute off does not stop its billing. To stop paying for a compute, destroy it. See [What you pay in each state](/services/compute/overview#what-you-pay-in-each-state).
</Warning>

## You pay in advance

Billing is prepaid. You top up your team, and each day we take the previous day's usage from your credits first, then from your funds. See [Paying your bill](/faqs/paying-your-bill).
