When someone signs up with your referral link and tops up enough, you both receive credits. The reward and the top-up amount that qualifies are shown on your **REFER & EARN** tab.

## Where your referral link is

Click your picture at the top of the dashboard, open **Account Settings** and go to the **REFER & EARN** tab. Copy the link and share it. Your referral code is part of the link.

The same tab lists everyone who signed up with your link, under **Your referrals**, with a status:

- **AWAITING TOPUP**: the person signed up but has not topped up enough yet.
- **REWARDED**: the person qualified.

There is no limit on how many people you refer.

## What counts as qualifying

The person you referred must top up teams they own. They qualify when the total of their top-ups reaches the amount shown on the tab. Signing up alone is not enough.

## How you receive the credits

When a referral qualifies, a dialog named **Referral Credits Earned** appears the next time you log in. Select the team that receives the credits and click **Apply Credits**.

<Warning>
  The dialog appears once, and it is the only time you are asked. Choose the team with care.
</Warning>

You can apply the credits only to a team you own at the moment the referral qualifies. A team you create afterwards cannot receive them.

The person you referred receives their credits the same way, on a team they own.

Referral credits are [credits](/faqs/paying-your-bill#credits-and-funds) like any other: they are spent before your funds, and they can have an expiry date. You see it in **Team Settings** > **Billing** > **Credits**.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I apply the credits to a team where I am an Admin or a Member?">
    No. You can apply them only to a team you own.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The person I referred topped up, but nothing happened. Why?">
    Their top-ups have not reached the qualifying amount yet. The reward comes when the total reaches it.
  </Accordion>

  </Accordion>
