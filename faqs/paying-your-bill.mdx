Strettch Cloud is prepaid. You top up your team, and each day we take the previous day's usage from your credits first, then from your funds.

Every team pays for itself, with its own credits and funds.

## Credits and funds

A team holds two amounts, and they are never added together.

- **Funds** are the money you topped up.
- **Credits** are amounts we add to a team: promotions, refunds, referral rewards, rewards for a long-term commitment, and compensation after an incident. A credit can have an expiry date.

Credits are spent first, starting with the credit that expires soonest. Your funds are used when the credits run out.

The number at the top of the dashboard shows your credits, labelled **Credits**, while you have any. After that it shows your funds, labelled **Balance**. **Team Settings** > **Billing** > **Overview** shows both, and the **Credits** tab lists each credit with its expiry date and how much of it is used.

## How to top up

Click **Top Up** at the top of the dashboard, enter an amount in RWF and click **Continue**. The smallest top-up is RWF 1,000. A payment window opens and shows the ways you can pay, such as mobile money and bank cards. Follow its steps.

When the payment is confirmed, your funds go up. Every top-up, with its status, is listed in **Team Settings** > **Billing** > **Transactions**.

## What is charged, and when

Once a day we charge the previous day's usage:

- **Computes** are charged by the hour, for as long as they exist. See [What you pay in each state](/services/compute/overview#what-you-pay-in-each-state).
- **Public IPs** are charged by the calendar month. See [What a public IP costs](/services/compute/public-ips-and-the-private-network#what-a-public-ip-costs).
- **Bandwidth** is charged only above your team's allowance. See [Bandwidth](/services/compute/bandwidth).

At the start of each month you get an [invoice](/faqs/invoices) that lists the charges of the month before.

## What happens when your team runs out

When usage is charged and your credits and funds do not cover it, your team owes the difference, and the month's invoice stays unpaid.

<Warning>
  If an invoice stays unpaid, we first turn your computes off, and later destroy them. Destroyed computes and their data cannot be brought back. Before each step we email every member of the team with the date it will happen.
</Warning>

While your team owes money, you cannot create new computes. After your computes are turned off, the dashboard asks you to top up at least the amount you owe before you can use it again.

To get a warning earlier, turn on **Low Balance Alert** in **Team Settings** > **Billing** > **Overview** and set an amount. We email every member of the team when your funds fall below it. An Owner or an Admin can set it.
