An invoice lists what your team used in one calendar month. We create it at the start of the next month. A team that used nothing in a month gets no invoice for it.

An invoice is not a request to pay again. Usage is already taken from your credits and funds each day. See [Paying your bill](/faqs/paying-your-bill).

## Who receives the invoice

We email the invoice as a PDF to the Owner of the team. To send it to more people, an Owner or an Admin adds their addresses in **Team Settings** > **General**, under **Contact Emails**.

## Where to see your invoices

Go to **Team Settings** > **Billing** > **Invoices**. Each row shows the invoice number, the issue date, the total and a status, **PAID** or **UNPAID**.

- Click **Invoice** on a row to download the PDF.
- For an invoice that is **UNPAID**, click **Pay Invoice**. You can pay that invoice or everything your team owes.

An invoice that stays unpaid leads to your team being suspended on the 15th and its computes being destroyed on the 29th. See [What happens when your team runs out](/faqs/paying-your-bill#what-happens-when-your-team-runs-out).

## What an invoice lists

- the billing period and the date of issue;
- who is billed;
- the total for the month, and the amount that is taxable;
- a breakdown of the usage behind the total.

Top-ups are not on an invoice. They are listed in **Team Settings** > **Billing** > **Transactions**.

All invoices are in Rwandan francs (RWF). We cannot change the date or the period of an invoice.

## Bandwidth on your invoice

Each team has a monthly allowance of outbound internet traffic, pooled from all its computes. If the team uses more than its allowance, the invoice has a **Bandwidth** section with one line, **Extra Bandwidth Usage**, showing the GiB above the allowance, the price per GiB and the amount. If the team stays within its allowance, there is no bandwidth line.

See [Bandwidth](/services/compute/bandwidth) for how the allowance and charges work.

## Tax receipts

An invoice is your billing statement. For a tax receipt for a paid invoice, see [Receipts](/faqs/receipts).
