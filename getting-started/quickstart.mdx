title: "Quickstart"
description: "Create a Strettch Cloud account, create your first compute, open a terminal on it in your browser, and destroy it again."
---

You create one small compute, open a terminal on it in your browser, and destroy it. You pay for the compute by the hour until you destroy it.

You need a phone number that can receive a code by SMS or WhatsApp, and a way to pay for your first top-up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [cloud.strettch.com](https://cloud.strettch.com/) and sign up with one of the sign-in providers on the page.

    On **Let's verify your phone number**, enter your number and click **Send Verification Code**. A Rwandan number (+250) can get the code by SMS or WhatsApp. Any other number gets it on WhatsApp. Enter the code.

    You are now in the dashboard, on **Computes**, in a team that we created for you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start a new compute">
    Click **Create Compute** and make these choices:

    - **Choose Location** and **Choose Image**: keep what is already selected.
    - **Choose Type**: choose the smallest specification you can select.
    - **Networking Options**: turn **Assign a Public IP Address** off. You do not need a public IP for this test, and a public IP is charged for the whole calendar month.
    - **Authentication Method**: keep **Root Password** and type a password that meets the rules under the field. Store it.
    - **Compute Name**: keep the name that is filled in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Top up and create">
    Click **Create Compute**.

    Your team must hold one month of the compute's price, although you pay only for the hours the compute exists. If it holds less, the **Top Up Credits** dialog opens with the amount that is missing. Click **Continue** and pay in the payment window. When the payment is confirmed, click **Create Compute** again.

    You are taken to the **Computes** list. The compute shows **STARTING...** and then **RUNNING**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check that it works">
    Click the compute. At the top of its page, click the console button, next to **Upsize**. While the button reads **Setting up your console**, wait.

    A new browser tab opens with a terminal, and you are logged in as `root`. Your compute works.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Destroy the compute">
    Destroy the compute so that billing stops. Open the **Destroy** tab, click **Destroy Compute**, type the text the dialog asks for and click **Destroy**.

    The funds you did not use stay in your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
