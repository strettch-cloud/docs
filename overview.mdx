Strettch Cloud is a cloud platform that runs in Rwanda. You rent computes, host the DNS of your domains and manage both with your team, and you pay in Rwandan francs.

Strettch is pronounced like "stretch". Strettch Cloud is a product of [strettch.com](https://strettch.com/).

## What you can use

- **[SC Compute](/services/compute/overview)**: virtual machines that run Linux. You reach them over SSH or from a terminal in your browser. The price of each specification is shown when you create a compute.
- **[DNS](/services/dns/overview)**: hosting for the DNS records of a domain you own.
- **[Teams](/services/teams/overview)**: every compute and domain belongs to a team. You invite people, give each one a role, and each team has its own bill.
- **[API](/api-reference/introduction)** and **[Terraform](/terraform/overview)**: create and manage computes from your own code.

## Why it runs in Rwanda

### Your users are closer

The closest region of the large cloud providers is in South Africa. From Rwanda, a request to the nearest AWS region, in Cape Town, takes about 110 ms, and up to 200 ms when the network is busy. An application makes many requests to load one page, so the delay adds up.

Strettch Cloud runs in Rwanda, which brings this down to 10 to 30 ms.

### You pay in your own currency

If you earn in Rwandan francs and pay for cloud in US dollars, your bill changes with the exchange rate. When the rate went from 1,200 to 1,500 RWF per dollar, a bill of 20,000 dollars a year went from 24 million to 30 million RWF with no change in usage.

Strettch Cloud prices and bills in Rwandan francs (RWF).

### Your data stays in the country

Some data must be stored inside the country by law. Computes on Strettch Cloud run in Rwanda, so what you store on them stays in Rwanda.

## Where it runs

Strettch Cloud has one region, Kigali, in Rwanda.

## How you pay

Billing is prepaid. You top up your team in Rwandan francs, and each day's usage is taken from what you have. See [Paying your bill](/faqs/paying-your-bill).
