## What to send us

The more of this you send in your first message, the sooner we can help.

**About a compute:**

- the name or ID of the compute, and its region
- the error message, or what you see in the web console
- what changed on the compute before the problem started
- when the problem started

**About DNS:**

- the domain
- the record type and the record you expected
- the nameservers set at your registrar
- how long ago you made the change

**About billing or your account:**

- the email address of your account and the name of your team
- the invoice number or the transaction ID

## What we help with

- Computes that do not start, cannot be reached or perform badly.
- Domains and DNS records hosted on Strettch Cloud.
- Public IPs and networking between your computes.
- Installing and repairing the SC Agent and the SC Metrics Agent.
- Billing, invoices, receipts and payments.
- Teams, members and access to your account.
- Security incidents that involve your Strettch Cloud resources.
- How a Strettch Cloud feature works.

## What we do not do

- Debug your application, its code or its dependencies.
- Configure software you install on a compute, such as a database or a web server.
- Administer the operating system inside your compute.
- Write code or scripts for you.
- Recover data from a compute you destroyed. Keep your own backups.

We may change how support works. If a change affects you, we tell you by email or in the dashboard.
